Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about LeadsPedia's platform, features, and implementation process.

How does LeadsPedia ensure lead quality and prevent fraud?

LeadsPedia employs advanced fraud detection algorithms and real-time validation to ensure lead quality. Our system checks for duplicate leads, verifies email addresses and phone numbers, and uses machine learning to identify suspicious patterns. We also offer customizable validation rules to match your specific requirements.

What integration options are available with LeadsPedia?

LeadsPedia offers multiple integration options including REST API, webhooks, and pre-built connectors for popular CRMs like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho. We also support Zapier integration for connecting with thousands of other applications, and our team can assist with custom integrations if needed.

How does the pricing work for LeadsPedia?

LeadsPedia offers flexible pricing based on your specific needs, including pay-per-lead and subscription-based models. Pricing depends on factors like lead volume, required features, and level of support. Contact our sales team for a customized quote based on your business requirements.

What kind of data security measures does LeadsPedia have in place?

LeadsPedia takes data security seriously. We use 256-bit SSL encryption for all data in transit and at rest. Our platform is SOC 2 Type II compliant, and we regularly undergo third-party security audits. We also offer data residency options and can sign Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) as needed.

Can I customize the lead distribution rules?

Yes, our platform offers highly customizable distribution rules. You can set up rules based on lead source, geographic location, lead score, time of day, and more. You can also create different rule sets for different campaigns or client requirements, with the ability to rotate or distribute leads based on percentage splits or round-robin distribution.

What kind of reporting and analytics are available?

LeadsPedia provides comprehensive reporting and analytics including lead source performance, conversion rates, ROI tracking, and more. You can generate custom reports, set up automated email reports, and view real-time dashboards to monitor your campaigns. Our advanced analytics also include lead scoring, conversion tracking, and ROI analysis to help you optimize your lead generation efforts.
